=== PAGE 18 ===
Threads of Tradition - A Sadu Weaving Workshop
Sadu weaving is one of Arabia's oldest and most cherished
art forms. In this hands-on workshop, Sit alongside a local
artisan to master an ancient craft woven from memory and
identity. You will decode the cultural meanings behind every
geometric pattern, learn traditional techniques using natural
materials, and weave your own piece of living Arabian history.
No experience needed - only curiosity.

=== PAGE 19 ===
From the Nature! A Palm Leaf Weaving Workshop
From the palm tree to your hands, this ancient craft carries
centuries of tradition in every twist and fold. Sit with a local
artisan who has dedicated her life to this art and learn how
palm leaves are carefully selected, dried, and shaped into
baskets, trays, and decorative pieces that have been part of
Arabian homes for generations. Create something meaningful
from nature itself and leave with a handcrafted piece made
entirely by your own hands.
