Website Pages — What Visitors Will See
50800044846
Your website will consist of 8 pages, all connected seamlessly. Below is each page, what it contains, and how it serves your clients.
Homepage
Full-screen cinematic hero, 'Who We Are' intro, featured destinations carousel, services grid, 4-step
process, client testimonials, and CTA banner.
✈Destinations
Saudi Arabia featured prominently (AlUla, Riyadh, Jeddah, NEOM), then GCC, then global. Each
destination: hero image, highlights, popular experiences, inquiry CTA.
Services
All 16 services displayed as premium cards — Private Jets, Umrah + Experiences, Luxury
Wellness, Visa Services, Chauffeur Services, Cruises, and more.
Featured Packages
Curated seasonal packages: destination, duration, highlights, from-price. Showcase only — visitors
are directed to the inquiry form to discuss their trip.
Blog / Travel Stories
Weekly articles on destinations, travel tips, and Saudi discoveries. Builds authority and keeps
visitors coming back. Managed from your private admin panel.
About Us
Your 20-year story, team, brand values, and global partner network. This is the trust page — luxury
clients research before they reach out.
Inquiry / Contact
A premium inquiry form capturing name, email, phone, travel dates, destination, group size, budget,
and message. WhatsApp button included. Instant notification to your team.
Admin Panel (Private)
Your private dashboard — write and publish blog posts, view all customer inquiries, manage
content, upload images. Accessible only by your authorised team.

07 Delivery Roadmap
50800044846
The project is delivered in five structured phases over 10–8 weeks. You will receive progress updates and can review work at the end of each phase.
Phase IProject Setup & FoundationWEEK 1–2
 Initial project setup and configuration
 Database tables created: Inquiries, Posts, Destinations, Packages, Services
 Security policies configured — customer data protected from day one
 Brand design tokens applied: brown, gold, beige palette
 GitHub repository connected to automatic deployment pipeline
Phase IICore Public PagesWEEKS
3–5
 Shared navigation header and footer
 Homepage: hero, Who We Are, destinations, services, process steps, testimonials, CTA
 About Us page
 Services page — all 16 services as premium cards
 Destinations pages: Saudi Arabia, GCC, and global
 Featured Packages listing page
 Contact / Inquiry page with working form and email notification
 Blog listing page
 Database seeded with initial destinations, services, and sample packages
PhaseAdmin Panel & Blog SystemWEEK 6–7
III
 Secure admin login — email and password
 Admin dashboard home with inquiry and post counts
 Blog post editor: create, edit, publish, delete with image upload
 Inquiries table with filtering, status updates, and CSV export
 Individual blog post pages with dynamic routing
PhaseSEO, Performance & PolishWEEK 8
IV
 SEO titles, descriptions, and social sharing images on every page
 Auto-generated sitemap and search engine configuration
 Image optimisation for fast load times
 Performance audit and fixes
 Accessibility review and mobile responsiveness final pass
Phase VTesting, Handoff & LaunchWEEK
9–10
 Cross-browser and mobile device testing
 End-to-end inquiry form test — form to dashboard to email
 Admin user guide created for your team
 Custom domain configured (yallavoyage.com)
 Post-launch monitoring set up
